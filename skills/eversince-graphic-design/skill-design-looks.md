# Graphic design: looks

Opened from `skill-graphic-design`. A look is named before the code or the canvas, anchored to a
reference a designer would recognize rather than to adjectives: a palette with roles, a pairing from
the font catalog, three display tiers, spacing, one signature, its moves, and the failure to hold
frames against. A brand's colors carry into the look that fits and keep its discipline. Percentages
are of the frame's width unless marked. Tiers are the three display sizes for a headline of three
words or fewer, four to six, and seven or more, measured within 78% of the width (`skill-graphic-design`).
Moves say how things arrive in a motion graphic (the ease family, entrance length, the one draw-on or
stamp, how scenes change), inside the motion rules of `skill-motion-graphics`; a still takes
everything but the moves.

A piece stays in one look by rotating a small set of frame kinds, each with its own density and
anchor: the opener (sparse, pinned left), the single statement, the section break (one huge numeral),
the figures frame (the busy one), the list (busy through repeated rows), the quote (centered and open),
the comparison (two halves, the winning half in the accent), the sequence, the sign-off (centered).

Where these sit in 2026, from designers' and foundries' trend reports: bold type, strong color blocks and raw neo-brutalist layouts are current (Placard, Copy shop, Sodium); expressive serifs and novel italics are back (Almanac, Harbor); quiet, muted minimalism is fading. Warped or liquid type and Y2K chrome are in fashion too, and date fastest. A look is chosen for the subject and the brand, not the trend.

## Almanac
A seed catalog, a small-press poetry annual. Patient, warm, lit from one side.
- Palette: linen #ECE5D3 ground (#E0D6BF a second surface); aubergine #2F1D35 for all type and every
  rule; saffron #E3A33A for the arch and nothing else; sage #93A88C for small marks only, never type.
- Type: Spectral 300 display in sentence case, line height 0.95 to 1.1, tracked -0.02em, one word in
  Spectral italic; Albert Sans 600 uppercase labels at 0.14 to 0.24em; figures in Albert Sans, tabular.
- Space: 5% edges, frames 50 to 60% empty; the list is the busy frame, rows parted by 2px rules.
- Signature: the arch, one flat saffron shape with a semicircular top, 30 to 38% of the width,
  standing on the bottom edge and cropped by it, off-center behind the focal. No gradient, outline or
  shadow, only ever one. A section break sets its numeral inside the arch at 24% of the width; a
  picture can sit in it as a window. A running head (title top left, folio top right, small uppercase)
  is the only chrome that persists.
- Tiers: 13.2 / 5.4 / 3.3; the numeral in the arch stands outside them.
- Moves: gentle, entrances 0.7 to 0.9s, words lifting a short way one after another; the arch rises
  out of the bottom edge over about 1.2s and holds. Scenes change on a half-second dissolve.
- Fails as: an arch used as a button or a border, or a warm template with ornament in every corner.

## Placard
A picket sign, a strike notice pasted to a wall. Blunt.
- Palette: chalk #EDEAE2 ground, tar #17150F type, vermilion #D9402B accent, stone #77726A quieter
  text, rules #CFCAC0. The statement frame flips to a tar ground with chalk type and rules #34302A;
  vermilion is the same on both and is never type below display size.
- Type: Big Shoulders 900 uppercase at -0.01em, line height 0.86, as tall as the frame allows while each line stays whole (text in a fixed box wraps rather than shrinks); Space
  Mono 700 uppercase at 0.1em for every label, date and count.
- Space: 5% edges with the type crowding the top; 40 to 50% empty; the tally is the busy frame.
- Signature: the correction. A phrase is set, struck through by a vermilion bar a sixth of the type
  size deep, and its replacement stands on the next line in tar. One per piece, on the frame that
  carries the argument. The tally sets points as tall numerals down the left, each claim beside its
  number and a 2px rule between rows; no bullets. A date stamp top right runs on content frames only.
- Tiers: 12 / 8.2 / 5.2, counted at two words or fewer, three to four, five or more.
- Moves: hard, entrances 0.3 to 0.45s, lines dropping into place; the bar draws across the struck
  phrase in 0.25s on the beat it is said and the replacement lands after it. Scenes hard-cut; the flip
  to tar is the one change of key.
- Fails as: three polite bullets under a logo.

## Copy shop
A photocopied gig flyer, a cut-and-paste newsletter. Noisy on the surface, ordered underneath.
- Palette: newsprint #E8E6DF ground; toner #121210 for type and blocks; highlighter #D4F03C as a flat
  swipe behind a word; marker blue #2F4FD8 for the one hand mark; tape #F1E7B8 at 85%; halftone gray
  #B9B6AD. Blue and highlighter never touch the same word.
- Type: Dela Gothic One uppercase, line height 0.95; Roboto Mono 500 uppercase labels at 0.08em;
  Permanent Marker for a single handwritten note per frame, four words at most.
- Space: 4% edges; pieces overlap but the focal keeps clear ground; 40 to 55% empty; the pasted-up
  figures are the busy frame.
- Signature: paste-up. Cards and pictures sit square or up to 2° off, each held by a strip of tape
  across a corner, 9% of the width and turned 30 to 40°. One word per frame gets a highlighter swipe,
  a flat block a little taller than the letters, turned -2°. One marker loop per frame rings a figure
  or a word. Toner blocks with newsprint type reversed out mark sections. No rounded corners, no soft
  shadows.
- Tiers: 14.2 (10.5 when the line runs to two) / 7.4 / 4.4.
- Moves: snap, entrances 0.25 to 0.4s; each piece drops onto the glass from slightly large, its tape
  lands a beat later, the swipe wipes left to right in 0.2s, the loop draws on over half a second.
  Scenes hard-cut.
- Fails as: a tidy card grid with a grunge texture laid over it.

## Prospectus
A shareholder letter, a private bank's research note. Calm and exact, for people who read the numbers.
- Palette: ivory #FBF8EE ground; graphite #1C1F22 headlines and body; deep teal #0F6A6E the one accent
  (a kicker, a key figure, a bar, the call to action); #5E6A6B secondary text; #8A9493 chrome only; up
  #2E7D4F and down #B83A30 only as change text beside a figure, never fills; rules #D9DDD6.
- Type: Geist and its mono alone: Geist 700 headlines at -0.02em, Geist 300 body, Geist Mono 500 for
  every figure, date and source, uppercase labels at 0.1em.
- Space: 6% edges; ordered, never crowded; the dashboard is the busy frame.
- Signature: the margin column. A column 22% wide, parted from the main column by one 2px rule, holds
  the kicker, the source and the change figures while the argument runs to its right. Data is a ladder
  of horizontal bars, teal or 20% teal, each value set at its bar's end: no pies, no cards, no shadows,
  no radius. A source line in Geist Mono closes every data frame.
- Tiers: 4.6 / 3.0 / 1.8, restrained on purpose and all above the message floor.
- Moves: measured, entrances 0.5 to 0.7s; the margin column arrives before the main one; bars grow out
  from the margin rule, longest first; figures count up. Scenes change on a short slide with a fade,
  never a cut mid-count.
- Fails as: a dashboard of rounded cards in five colors.

## Survey
An ordnance map sheet, a geological survey plate. One ink, exact, unhurried.
- Palette: buff #F2E9D8 ground; oxide #9E3B22 for everything; #B5654C for subtitles at display size
  only; contour lines at 14% oxide, edge ticks at 40%. Never a second hue.
- Type: Saira Condensed 800 uppercase headlines at 0.01em against Saira Condensed 300 subtitles and
  body, the hierarchy in the jump of weight; DM Mono for coordinates, figures and labels.
- Space: 5% inside the tick border; declarative frames 50 to 60% empty with the contours showing;
  tables and plotted data are the busy frames.
- Signature: the survey border, fine ticks on all four edges every 5% of the width with a coordinate
  at every fourth, is the chrome that never leaves. Behind declarative frames sit six to nine nested
  contour lines, irregular closed 2px paths, centered off the focal. Data is plotted as dots on
  crosshairs, a key value flagged with a small triangle. A scale bar bottom left carries the section
  number.
- Tiers: 11 (13 for a numeral) / 6.2 / 4.
- Moves: steady, entrances around 0.6s; contours draw on from the innermost outward 0.08s apart;
  plotted dots appear in a sweep left to right; the border never moves and carries across every cut.
  Scenes hard-cut.
- Fails as: a colorful map with pins, or a second ink.

## Terrace
A football program, a matchday poster on a stadium wall. Proud, loud, local.
- Palette: claret #6D1E34 and sky #9CC7E6 as the club pair, bone #F3EEE4 ground, night #17202B type on
  sky and bone, stone #6F6A66 small meta on bone. Type on claret is bone. Claret and sky meet only
  along a sash edge.
- Type: Antonio 700 uppercase at 0.02em, line height 0.9, figures included; Work Sans 400 body and 600
  uppercase labels at 0.12em.
- Space: 5% edges; 40 to 55% empty; the line-up, a numbered list in two columns, is the busy frame.
- Signature: the sash. One band crosses the frame at -8°, 22 to 30% of the height deep, in claret or
  sky, running off both edges with the headline along it; either side holds a picture or bone ground.
  The shirt number: a numeral at 40% of the height drawn in outline only, a 3px stroke in the sash
  color, behind a figure or a name. A frame carries the sash or a full-size shirt number, never both.
- Tiers: 11.5 / 7 / 4.8.
- Moves: the sash wipes in along its own angle over 0.45 to 0.6s; the shirt number strokes on behind
  it a beat later; names slide in the sash's direction. Scenes change on a diagonal wipe at the sash
  angle, the one transition.
- Fails as: a sports template of gradients, lens flares and a metal crest.

## Handbook
An engineering handbook with real code in it, a studio's style manual. Plain, well made, trusted.
- Palette: stone #EEF0EA ground; tile #E1E5DC a step darker where content gathers; ink #1E2421;
  tomato #D8553A once per frame (the call to action, one word, one marker), never a fill or a
  headline; code on #1C2320 with syntax in tomato, seafoam #7CC4A8 and mustard #D8B04A, comments
  #7B857F.
- Type: Lora 500 display in sentence case at -0.015em, italic for a definition or an aside; IBM Plex
  Sans 400 body; IBM Plex Mono for code, labels and figures, uppercase labels at 0.12em. A figure is
  mono and its unit is the sans at half the size.
- Space: 4.5% edges; density follows the content around one clear focal.
- Signature: annotation. A 2px ink leader ending in a 6px dot ties a margin label to the exact line
  or part it explains; line numbers run in the code gutter at 40% ink; surfaces part by tone alone,
  tile on stone, with 4px corners and no shadow or border.
- Tiers: 9.2 / 6.6 / 4.2, the top two kept for the opener, a statement and a figure.
- Moves: gentle entrances 0.5 to 0.7s and no overshoot; code appears a line at a time; a leader draws
  from its label once the target is in place; figures count up. Scenes change on short dissolves.
- Fails as: pure white and cool gray, drop shadows, or two accents competing.

## Playroom
A cut-paper picture book, a classroom wall. Bright and friendly, never babyish.
- Palette: warm white #FBF6EC ground; plum #2E2440 for all type; paper colors tomato #EE6A4D,
  cornflower #7A9CE8, mustard #F2C14E and leaf #7FB77E, interchangeable, one large shape per frame in
  each at most. Type on a paper color is plum, never white.
- Type: Rubik 800 display in sentence case at -0.01em, Rubik 400 body; Caveat 600 for one handwritten
  label per frame.
- Space: 5% edges; one content block per frame ringed by shapes cropped at the frame's edge; 40 to
  50% empty.
- Signature: cut paper. Flat blobs, circles and uneven rounded rectangles with no outlines. Depth is
  a second copy of the shape in its own color taken 20% toward plum, offset 1% down and right, never
  a black or blurred shadow. Three to six shapes per frame, larger at the edges and cropped by them;
  content cards are warm white with the same paper depth. Emphasis in a line is a hand-drawn squiggle
  under the word, never bold.
- Tiers: 7.2 / 5 / 3.4.
- Moves: the one bouncy look. Content settles; only the paper shapes pop on with a small overshoot,
  staggered, turning in by 6° at most. Scenes change as one large paper shape sweeps across the frame.
- Fails as: a flat corporate slide with emoji stuck on.

## Harbor
A shipping line's annual report, a maritime museum's wall label. Composed, weathered, serious.
- Palette: harbor navy #1F2E3D ground (#172431 a second surface); fog #DCE2E1 headlines and body;
  brass #BF9443 the accent (a rule, a figure, the seal); slate #8497A6 secondary text; rust #B4553A a
  small marker only, never type. Content frames may invert to a fog ground with navy type, where brass
  deepens to #86652A.
- Type: Libre Baskerville 400 display at -0.01em, 700 for a figure; Hanken Grotesk 400 body and 600
  uppercase labels at 0.16em. No italic in display.
- Space: 5.5% edges; a single subject in wide open space, 50 to 60% empty; a table of routes or
  figures in 2px-ruled rows is the busy frame.
- Signature: the waterline and the seal. A full-width 2px brass rule at 62% of the height; the
  headline stands on it and everything supporting sits below, and only a picture crosses it. On the
  opener and the close only, a seal: two concentric 2px brass rings with a numeral at the center and
  the piece's name in spaced capitals beneath. A running foot bottom left names the section.
- Tiers: 10.4 / 5.6 / 4, the top tier kept for the opener, a statement and a figure.
- Moves: settle, entrances 0.7 to 0.9s; the waterline draws first, left to right; the seal's rings
  draw on and its name follows; figures count up. Scenes hard-cut between navy and fog, or dissolve
  slowly.
- Fails as: a nautical template of anchors and rope, or a glossy blue corporate deck.

## Sodium
A street at night under sodium lamps, the cold open of a crime drama. Dark, cinematic, withheld.
- Palette: night #0C0F14 ground, asphalt #1E232B lit surfaces, slate #6A7280 for what recedes, cold
  white #EDF0F3 for what matters, sodium #E8912F the one warm light, at most one use a frame and often
  none.
- Type: Barlow Condensed 800 uppercase display at +0.02em against Barlow Condensed 300 for the setup;
  Fira Code for a time, a plate or a reference number.
- Tiers: 12.5 / 7.8 / 5; condensed capitals hold more words at a size.
- Space: 55 to 70% of the frame in the dark; the focal sits in a lit band.
- Signature: the lit band. The focal is never faded up out of black; a hard-edged band of asphalt
  opens around the line from a 2px slit, with a faint sodium cast of 8 to 12% across it. The type
  inside is already in place and does not move. One band per frame, and a held beat of dark before the
  line that matters.
- Moves: the band opens over 0.5 to 0.7s, a scale from 1.02 to 1 at most on what it reveals; never a
  creeping push. Scenes change by the band closing to its slit and cutting to dark.
- Fails as: gray haze with nothing lit, or orange light everywhere.
