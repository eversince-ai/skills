# Eversince skills

Eversince is the media workspace for agents, where a user and their agents make content together. These are its skills. Each holds how a professional does one kind of media job (editing recordings, clips, captions, sound, motion graphics, thumbnails, carousels, ads, generation and more): the decisions, the numbers that matter and the calls in order, written for an agent to read before it starts. They run on Eversince's tools.

```bash
npx skills add eversince-ai/skills
```

The installer copies them into the agent's skills folder. The `eversince` skill says when Eversince fits and how to connect it; https://docs.eversince.ai has the rest. The Eversince CLI and desktop app bring installed copies to the latest version once a day, and a connected agent reads each skill's current version through `manage_skills`.

This repo is generated from the skills Eversince serves to connected agents; changes made here are replaced by the next release.

## Skills

- `eversince`: when Eversince fits the work, and how to connect it
- `eversince-make-content`: Running content with or for a user: their goals, research into what works, a strategy, making it, review, and publishing.
- `eversince-make-videos`: Editing recordings: what is possible (cutting, sound, captions, framing, look), checking in proportion, and the skills by footage.
- `eversince-make-images`: Thumbnails, carousels, post images, posters, edited photos, product shots and AI pictures: how each is made and its skill.
- `eversince-promote`: Videos and images that sell a product, release, event or service: what to know first, which kind fits, and the skill for each.
- `eversince-video-editing`: Raw footage into a finished video, following what is said: read all, the story, a loose cut, passes, levels and captions. Clips: clipping.
- `eversince-edit-in-my-style`: The user's own finished videos read into a written editing style, new footage cut to it and compared.
- `eversince-remake-from-a-reference`: A video like a reference, with the user's footage: the reference written down as numbers, built, compared at the same seconds.
- `eversince-short-form-videos`: A Reel, Short or TikTok around one idea: the shape, beats, safe area, sound and rights, assembly, checks. A video that sells: UGC ads.
- `eversince-clipping`: Finds the moments in something long and cuts each to stand alone as a set of clips. One finished video from the footage: video editing.
- `eversince-podcast-editing`: A podcast episode, video or audio: the cut follows whoever talks, pauses and fillers trimmed, sound leveled, chapters, an audio master.
- `eversince-animated-captions`: Word-timed captions styled to fit the video: the look chosen from it, lines, timing, emphasis, readable over any footage.
- `eversince-vlog-editing`: A day or trip cut into one story in the order it happened: a short day in my life, a long YouTube vlog, a travel or silent vlog.
- `eversince-screen-recording-editing`: A screen recording into a demo someone can follow: dead time cut, zooms on the actions, each step named, the interface kept true.
- `eversince-sound-design`: A video's sound mixed to the cut: voice cleaned, music ducked, effects, silence, levels, cutting to music. New voice or track: voiceovers.
- `eversince-color-grading`: Corrects shots to one reference, then one look over the whole video. For a cut whose pictures do not match, or a grade.
- `eversince-translation-and-dubbing`: A finished video in another language: a dub in the same voices, or subtitles, fitted to the cut and checked. Lines alone are captions.
- `eversince-music-videos`: A performance, footage, photos or type cut to a song: takes synced to the track, cuts on the beat, lyrics on screen.
- `eversince-smart-glasses-footage`: Clips from camera glasses into a short first-person video: what to keep and cut, the 3:4 to 9:16 crop, sound and captions.
- `eversince-video-effects`: Named effects on a video: text behind a person, a new background, speed ramps, shatter, a 3D screen, designed transitions.
- `eversince-motion-graphics`: Graphics as code on the timeline, Remotion JSX or HyperFrames HTML: titles, lower thirds, charts, kinetic type, scenes.
- `eversince-explainer-videos`: A narrated video that makes one idea understood, beat by beat, with motion graphics, footage, stock or generated pictures.
- `eversince-making-thumbnails`: The one image that sells a video or post: a thumbnail, poster or cover, judged at the size it runs. An ad with copy: image ads.
- `eversince-making-carousels`: A carousel for a feed, or a deck for a room: one idea and one visual per slide, a cover that stops the scroll, one design.
- `eversince-graphic-design`: A post, poster or cover in editable layers, and the type, color and composition for anything seen: a still, a slide, a frame.
- `eversince-photo-editing`: A photograph finished the way a photographer would: straighten, light, color, a filter or look, looks to choose from; one photo or a set.
- `eversince-product-photography`: Photos of a product from the real product, finished or generated: packshot, cutout, lifestyle, detail, scale, family.
- `eversince-brand-style`: A brand's colors, fonts, logos and look, read from what the user has, agreed, and saved in the brand kit for everything made later.
- `eversince-social-trends`: What is taking off in a field now, from its top posts: each trend's stage, fit and the user's angles on it. One account is account audit.
- `eversince-account-audit`: One account's posts studied against its own median: what breaks out, what holds back, what to change, its style. A field is social trends.
- `eversince-content-ideas`: Ideas for the next posts, each traced to evidence (an outlier, a search, a question), with hook and format. A field's trends: social trends.
- `eversince-hooks-and-scripts`: The words of a video before it is made: one idea, the hook as picture, line and text, beats and length, the script in two columns.
- `eversince-content-calendar`: Running posts on the workspace calendar, planned ahead or placed when ready: entries, captions, statuses, the hand-off to posting.
- `eversince-run-ads`: Ads launched, read and improved: what each creative did and why, then left, ended or remade from its source. Making a set is ad variations.
- `eversince-image-ads`: An image ad built on one angle, chosen by what the buyer already knows: headline, product, layout, sized for each placement.
- `eversince-ad-variations`: New versions of an ad that works, or a set to test: angles first, each variant one stated change a platform sees as different.
- `eversince-ugc-ads`: A creator-style video ad, to camera or over footage, from real takes or generated. A set of variants: ad variations.
- `eversince-product-videos`: The film where the product is the hero and the picture carries it: reveal, detail run, demonstration, few words or none.
- `eversince-launch-videos`: A narrated case for a product, a release or an update, with real screens as proof: one claim, clear by the second beat, one ask.
- `eversince-teasers-and-trailers`: A teaser or trailer that makes a release wanted without explaining it, cut to its track, landing on the title and where to get it.
- `eversince-content-repurposing`: One finished video re-edited for each platform: its shape, length, opening and captions, each a version beside the original.
- `eversince-publishing`: A video or image made ready to post: each platform's file and text, the time, then sent or scheduled to connected accounts or handed over.
- `eversince-inbox-and-auto-replies`: Comments and messages answered in the account's voice, and the comment-a-word reply that sends a link to everyone who asks.
- `eversince-public-links`: Media, text or both on a page anyone opens without an account, for clients, an audience or teammates: what it holds and what a plan adds.
- `eversince-keeping-content-organized`: A workspace structure agreed with the user and held every session: projects, labels, names, a calendar; media in and found.
- `eversince-searching-your-backlog`: The user's past content wherever it lives (their channels, Drive, a computer) brought in with them, then searched.
- `eversince-ai-filmmaking`: AI-generated video that looks like film: how shots, light, movement and coverage are designed and prompted. Drawn frames are animation.
- `eversince-ai-animation`: AI-generated animation: drawn, painted or rendered worlds, style families, motion, one style held across shots. Type is motion graphics.
- `eversince-ai-influencer`: An AI person or character made once, face and voice, and kept identical in every scene: the reference set, generating from it, posting it.
- `eversince-transform-video`: Real footage changed by a video model, the performance kept 1:1: a new angle, light, location, outfit or object, one change a pass.
- `eversince-faceless-videos`: A channel with nobody on camera: the topic, one format kept episode to episode, voice and pictures, and staying clear of mass-produced.
- `eversince-voiceovers-and-music`: A script read in a chosen voice and a track made to the length and mood of the video, every take kept. The mix itself is sound design.
- `eversince-finish`: The last pass over a whole video before it leaves: rhythm, restraint, consistency, the first and last frame, read across the cut.
- `eversince-timeline-operations`: Reference for edit_timeline operations: every field, unit and default, effects, grade, masks, transitions, keyframes and camera moves.
- `eversince-canvas-operations`: Reference for edit_canvas operations: every layer kind and its fields, boxes, masks, grades, slides and limits.
